Quant research
at Genk Capital

  • Derived a microstructure signal for price-level collapses in large-tick assets: a consistent 19-point lift in collapse persistence across every flow regime (40% to 59% in the tradable regime).
  • Modelled passive spread-capture entries as three outcomes (fill, adverse selection, flat) and showed positive net expected value at every queue position after taker fees.
  • Survival analysis on 605K Level-2 updates: a 97 ms median time-to-collapse, fast but usable in real time.
  • Parameter sweeps across regimes and instruments showed fill probability as the main driver of expected value.
